How I work

From ambiguity to a useful next version.

I favor fast learning loops over oversized plans. Understand the problem deeply, build something small enough to test, then improve it with evidence.

Discover

Clarify the real objective, constraints, stakeholders, and what success actually means.

Map the System

Identify inputs, bottlenecks, feedback loops, dependencies, and the highest-leverage points.

Build the Smallest Useful Version

Create an actionable prototype, plan, workflow, or coaching intervention that can produce real feedback.

Measure & Iterate

Use outcomes—not attachment to the original idea—to decide what should change next.

About

I work best where disciplines overlap.

David Gregory Wilson is a coach, systems integrator, researcher, and builder with more than two decades of hands-on experience helping people improve performance. His current work combines human performance, AI-assisted research, knowledge architecture, education, and practical systems design.

He is most useful where a problem crosses multiple domains and needs to be translated into a clear next move. The approach is simple: stay curious, reason from first principles, separate evidence from assumption, and keep moving toward useful execution.
